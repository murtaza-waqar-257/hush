Questions

Before you try it

Will callers know it's AI?

It sounds natural, but if someone asks, Hush tells them it's an automated assistant. Most callers just want the problem sorted.

What about emergencies?

You choose what counts as urgent. For those, Hush can text you straight away so you can call back fast.

Do I need a new phone number?

No. You forward calls from your existing number, so nothing changes for your customers.

What happens after the free week?

Nothing, unless you want to keep it. If you do, we'll agree a simple monthly price that fits your business.
