Privacy policy

How Hush handles your information

Last updated: 14 November 2026

In short: if you sign up for the trial, I keep your name, business name, mobile number and how many calls you miss. I use them only to contact you about Hush. I never sell your details or add you to marketing lists, and this website doesn't use cookies or tracking.

Who I am

Hush is run by Mohammad Murtaza Waqar in the United Kingdom. I'm responsible for your personal information under UK data protection law (the UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018).

For any privacy question or request, email contact@hushdesk.app.

What I collect from this website

When you fill in the free trial form, I collect:

  • your name
  • your business name
  • your UK mobile number
  • roughly how many calls you miss a week

To stop spam, I also keep a scrambled (hashed) code made from your internet connection's address, never the address itself. It's only used to limit repeat sign-ups and is deleted after 24 hours.

Nothing else is collected. There are no cookies, analytics or advertising trackers on this site.

Why I use it

I use your details only to contact you about the free trial and to set Hush up for your business. The legal basis is that you asked me to contact you, so I'm taking steps at your request before we agree to work together (UK GDPR Article 6(1)(b)).

Who else handles it

I use a few trusted services to run Hush. Each one only gets what it needs:

  • Supabase stores trial sign-ups in its London data centre.
  • FormSubmit emails me a copy of each sign-up so I can reply quickly. It may process the email outside the UK.
  • GitHub Pages hosts this website. Like any web host, it may log your internet address when you visit.
  • Google Fonts supplies the fonts on this page, so Google receives your internet address when the page loads.

Where a service is based outside the UK, it relies on UK-approved safeguards for international transfers.

Calls answered by Hush

When Hush answers calls for a pest control business, it collects the caller's details (such as name, number, address and the pest problem) and sends them to that business. The pest control business decides how that information is used and is responsible for it. Hush handles it on their behalf, using a voice AI provider to hear and answer the call. Callers who want to ask about their information should contact the business they rang.

How long I keep it

Trial sign-ups are deleted automatically after 12 months. If you ask me to delete your details sooner, I'll do it within a month.

How it's protected

Sign-ups are stored in a private area of the database that can't be read from this website. The form can only add new sign-ups; it can't look anything up. Only I can see the stored details.

Your rights

You can ask me to:

  • give you a copy of the information I hold about you
  • correct anything that's wrong
  • delete your information
  • stop using your information

Email contact@hushdesk.app and I'll reply within one month. It's free.

If you're unhappy with how I've handled your information, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk. I'd appreciate the chance to put things right first.

Changes

If this policy changes, I'll update the date at the top of this page.
